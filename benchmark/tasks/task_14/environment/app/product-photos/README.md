# Product Photos

This directory contains high-resolution product images from our photographer.

## Image Specifications

- **Resolution**: 5000x5000 pixels
- **Format**: JPEG (products) and PNG (logo)
- **Quality**: Professional grade, uncompressed

## Files

- `product_1.jpg` - Premium Product 1
- `product_2.jpg` - Premium Product 2
- `product_3.jpg` - Premium Product 3
- `product_4.jpg` - Premium Product 4
- `product_5.jpg` - Premium Product 5
- `product_logo.png` - Company logo

## Usage

These images are used in:
- Website product pages (see `index.html`)
- Marketing materials
- Product catalogs
- Social media posts

## Notes

⚠️ **Important**: These images are too large for web use. They need to be resized to 1200px width and compressed before uploading to the website.

For web-ready versions, see the `web-ready/` folder after processing.
